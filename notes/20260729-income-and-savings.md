## how income and savings should work

1. income is money coming in, so it does not have a bucket
2. salary and tax returns are income categories, not savings categories. expenses use the needs, wants or savings buckets, so the user must log their income for each month
3. if there is no income, show `log income to see your monthly allocation`
4. the monthly ratios are calculated from the income logged that month
5. anything left after needs and wants is automatically counted as savings
6. negative savings means the user spent over their income in that month 
7. starting savings is what the user had before using the app. to show a final accurate number
8. current savings = starting savings + income - needs - wants
9. the numbers are only accurate if the user logs all their transactions
